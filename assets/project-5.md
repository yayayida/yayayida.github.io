# Mobility Patterns in Loures and Vila Franca
**Sep 2024 – Nov 2024 · Lisbon, Portugal**
Question: How do socio-demographic characteristics influence residents’ choice of transport
mode in Loures and Vila Franca?
- Mobility patterns: Social Demographic (gender/age/occupancy/education/income)
distribution in modal choice; OD trips; Trip purposes/frequency/duration per mode;
Perceived costs and quality (speed, safety, convenience) of private versus public transpor
- Spatial accessibility - especially walking distance to bus stops
