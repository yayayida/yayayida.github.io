# Signalized Intersection Improvement
**Nov 2024 – Jan 2024 · Lisbon, Portugal**
- Analysis current signal phase and design of intersection in Lisboa street and count the traffic flow at the experiment
- Compute Saturation flow rates, Delays per approach, LOS per approach, Average delays using HCM methedology 
- Compare the estimated intersection type and compute the improved approach to reduce the LOS of approaches
