# Urban Expansion Scenario in Moita
**Nov 2024 – Feb 2025 · Lisbon, Portugal**

Portugal is facing a housing crisis, marked by rising rents, a shortage of affordable housing, and a decline in new housing construction​. This research aims to explore the potential impact of law 117/2024 on the parish of Moita, and if affordability goals will be reached/

## Highlights
- Demographic Development in Moita​
- Determining the Need for Urban Expansion in Moita
- Scenario 1: Mainly dense multifamily, some reasonably dense single-family houses​; Proximity to existing urban cores, commerce and PT, especially the train stations​; ~27 ha multi-family, ~27 ha single family​
- Scenario 2: Mainly single-family homes in small density areas​; Some developments adjacent to other urban areas, some not​; ~5,5 ha multi-family, ~211 ha single-family​
