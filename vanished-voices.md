<div class="bilingual-pair bilingual-pair--abstract">
  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">摘要：《申报》作为近代上海舆论媒介的典型代表，无论在叙事特征还是文本信息方面都具有重要的历史研究价值，然而《申报》中刊登的妓女新闻却是大陆现有学术研究中鲜为涉及的主题。通过研究《申报》中的妓女新闻，既可以了解近代报纸塑造女性形象时的叙事意识和社会语境，也可以从文本信息探究其背后折射的娼妓业问题，为现有娼妓业研究提供可资借鉴的历史信息。本文对1928-1937年《申报》妓女新闻的内容、女性形象及其关联的叙事特征进行分析，并从娼妓业整体特征、娼妓业内部的妓女群体和娼妓业外部的社会伦理问题三个方面讨论《申报》妓女新闻展现的娼妓业信息，力求全面挖掘该十年间《申报》妓女新闻的内容、特征、信息和历史价值，补充学术界较少关注的下属群体研究方向，为《申报》报刊研究和近代上海娼妓业研究提供有效的思想资源。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">Abstract: <i>Shen Bao</i>, a representative example of the modern Shanghai media, has important historical research value in terms of narrative features and text messages, yet the publication of prostitutes in Shen Bao is a subject of little academic research on the continent. The study of the Prostitutes in Shen Bao provides both an insight into the narrative and social context in which the modern newspapers portray women and a search for the repertoire of prostitution from textual information, providing historical information to inform existing research on prostitution. This paper analyses the content of the 1928-1937 Shen Bao of Prostitutes, the image of women and the narratives associated with it, and discusses the information on the prostitution of prostitutes presented in Shen Bao of Prostitutes in the context of the overall characteristics of the prostitution trade, the groups of prostitutes within the prostitution trade and the social ethics of the prostitution trade outside the prostitution trade. It seeks to fully exploit the content, characteristics, information and historical value of Shen Bao of Prostitutes over the decade, supplementing the research orientation of the less interested groups in academia, and provides an effective resource for the study of the Shen Bao and the research of the recent Shanghai Prostitutes.</p>

<div class="bilingual-pair">
  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">本文选取1928-1937年作为《申报》妓女新闻的研究时段，原因有二：（1）1928-1937年是上海娼妓业发展进程的典型时期，《申报》作为实时反映社会新闻的重要媒体，在这十年间刊登的妓女新闻数量较多，最具有典型性；（2）1928-1937年是《申报》发展最兴旺、最具备研究价值的阶段。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">The time period for the study of the prostitution news as the Shen Bao, 1928-1937, is selected for two reasons: (1) 1928-1937 was a typical period in the development of the Shanghai sex trade, and Shen Bao was the most typical and significant medium for real-time social news, with a high number of prostitutes news stories published during the decade; (2) The period 1928-1937 was the most dynamic and of most research value in the development of Shen Bao.</p>

<div class="bilingual-pair">
  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">19世纪中期，鸦片战争打开了上海的市场，繁荣的贸易与近代化浪潮的冲击使上海率先步入都市化进程。人口迁徙引起的社会结构变迁不断解构与重构女性的角色定位，消费主义的风靡成为推动社会结构流动性的一股洪流，大大加速了传统士大夫和高等妓女角色的式微，上海名妓的“黄金时代”<sup id="ref-zh-1"><a href="#note-1" aria-label="Footnote 1">1</a></sup>悄然落幕。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">In the middle of the nineteenth century, the opium war opened up the market in Shanghai, where booming trade and the impact of recent waves of urbanization led to the urbanization process. The social transformations resulting from migrations have been deconstructing and re-establishing the role of women, and the culture of consumerism has become a flood that has driven the mobility of social structures, greatly accelerating the decline in traditional doctorial and senior prostitutes&#x27; roles, and the “golden age” of Shanghai prostitutes has come to a close.</p>

  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">By the end of the nineteenth century, “the market for prostitution in Shanghai had evolved from a luxury market dominated by a small group of high-ranking women, designed to meet the needs of the 19th century urban elite, to a market that provided sexual services to an increasing number of unmarried men in the urban business class” <sup id="ref-en-2"><a href="#note-2" aria-label="Footnote 2">2</a></sup>, the civilianization of sexual resources, the marketization of sexual resources, the stimulation of supply demand, the breakdown of romantic love ideology, the entry of large numbers of women into the sex industry chain, which had become the lower-threshold labour force, and the decline of sex goods, accompanied by a significant increase in the number of prostitutes (unlicensed street-maided women).   <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">In the Declared Prostitute News, the image of women is often fragmented.   <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">The image of women in the news reveals what the narrative features, why it is used, and how the narrative of prostitutes affects our reading of the Prostitutes&#x27; News in Shen Bao is a question that is worth thinking.</p>

  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">因此，贞操问题在婚恋市场的影响不可一概而论。在1928-1937十年间，婚恋观处于新旧交杂的状态，报纸舆论不断传播婚姻自由的声音，老辈的封建思想则举着“贞节牌坊”顽强扎根在土壤里，身处这一时段的年青人往往处于进退两难的境地。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">The impact of virginity in the marriage market cannot therefore be considered as a whole. During the decade of 1928-1937, the concept of marriage was in a new and old state, with newspaper opinion spreading the voice of freedom of marriage, while the feudal ideology of elders, who had taken root in the soil with the chastity shop, was often in a dilemma for young people at that time.</p>
</div>
<div class="bilingual-pair">
  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">相比婚恋市场观念的冲突与融合，娼妓业市场里的贞节问题则严重许多。正规妓院里，“小先生”的初夜叫“开苞”、“破瓜”、“点大蜡烛”，开苞事宜由买者和老鸨商议，费用约在五百至上千元之间。<sup id="ref-zh-49"><a href="#note-49" aria-label="Footnote 49">49</a></sup>鸨母们为了丰厚的开苞费用，通常会严加看管甚至监禁雏妓，绝不让她们在无人陪伴的情况下外出，雏妓们连吃饭、上厕所也要被干涉。<sup id="ref-zh-50"><a href="#note-50" aria-label="Footnote 50">50</a></sup>嫖客若发现小先生并非处女，无论对嫖客还是妓女而言都是一种羞辱。1931年4月25日刊登的新闻《梳妆后谓非处女，妓女羞愤自杀》讲述了一名妓女因初夜被狎客称谓非处女而羞愤自杀的故事，新闻中写“（妓女）无从分辩，……深自怨艾，以耻辱太甚，何颜见人，遽萌自杀之念”，该妓女的父亲以“我虽不杀伯仁，伯仁实由我而死”之由控诉狎客。从该新闻描述可知：（1）贞节对妓院小先生的重要程度不亚于生命；（2）狎客对贞节的判断只需空口下定论，产生的结果却无任何容错率。新闻中女子“无从分辨”、极度羞愤，很可能是狎客误判所致，但由于此事无法证伪，妓女有冤无处伸张，空口判断可以毁了妓女一生。其父控诉也仅能从道德上指控狎客，而无法从案情本身是否冤屈角度指控。因此，贞节问题不仅是小先生们的枷锁，也是狎客们欺压妓女的利器。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">The problem of virginity in the prostitution market is much more serious than the conflict and integration of the concept of the marriage market. In the regular brothel, the first night of the day, “Small” is called “Crunch”, “Big Gourd”, “Done Candle”, which is negotiated between the buyer and the Laos and costs between $500 and $1,000. The news, &quot;No virgin after the dress, No Virtue for the Prostitute &quot;, published on 25 April 1931, tells the story of a prostitute who was ashamed to commit suicide because he was called a virgin by a prostitute, writing in the news that “there is no way to explain (the prostitute) is so angry, too ashamed, how to look, how to commit suicide”, and the prostitute&#x27;s father was accused of “I did not kill Beren, and he died by me”. His father &#x27; s complaint could also only be morally accusing a client and not in terms of whether the case was per se unfair. Thus, the issue of chastity is not only a shackle for young men, but also a tool for the abuse of prostitutes by the traffickers.</p>
</div>
<div class="bilingual-pair">

In between words, while not specifying what kind of prostitutes society should promote, it implicitly divides prostitutes into “graceful” and “fast” and directs readers to think “what is a good woman among prostitutes” and “what is a bad woman among prostitutes” through emotional tendencies in the words. Thus, the so-called biography is not in any sense a comprehensive record of life, but a series of stories intended to express and evaluate the character of prostitutes. The newspaper&#x27;s news coverage is the same. The narratives of events and evaluations are merely “performance and evaluation” at the bottom of the equation, and the sensitive issue of chastity is the central tool for presenting the dichotomy. Prostitutes are squeezed into randomly defined figures, their voices are weak in the public sphere, no one will be able to interact with “physically decorated” sub-groups, while tools chosen by the powerful — virtue, appearance, talent — become a heavy mountain on small individuals.</p>

  <h2 class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">四、结语</h2>
  <h2 class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">IV. Conclusion</h2>
</div>
<div class="bilingual-pair">
  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">1928-1937年是上海娼妓业解构与重构的冲突期，也是《申报》新闻走向巅峰的黄金时期。从《申报》在这一时段刊登的妓女新闻中，我们可以解读出当时媒体叙事下的妓女形象，及其符号背后的文化意义；同时，《申报》作为有价值的历史研究资料，我们也可以从中解读出当时娼妓业的发展概况、妓女群体在传统史料中被掩盖的声音、贞节问题在娼妓业和社会中的冲突对比。研究《申报》妓女新闻既是对如今《申报》研究和娼妓业研究材料的重要补充，也是在引导人们关注时代下属群体，打破传统思维和固有叙事里的认知，倾听与挖掘她们“消失的声音”。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">The years 1928-1937 were periods of conflict between the deconstruction and reconstruction of the Shanghai prostitutes and the golden period of the peak of Shen Bao. From the news of the prostitute published in Shen Bao during this period, we can interpret the image of the prostitute under the media narrative at the time and the cultural significance behind the symbol; At the same time, Shen Bao is a valuable historical study from which we can interpret the development of the prostitution trade at that time, the voices of the prostitutes&#x27; group that have been hidden in the traditional history, the conflict between the chastity issue in the prostitution trade and society. The publication of Shen Bao of Prostitutes is an important complement to the present-day work on Shen Bao and the research on prostitution, and it is also a guide to the contemporary sub-groups, breaking the traditional mindset and the inherent narratives and listening to and tapping into their “disappeared voices”.</p>
</div>
<div class="bilingual-pair">
  <p class="bilingual-pair__zh" lang="zh-CN">这对现代历史学研究也有重要的启示意义。由于时代限制，历史中很难找到下属群体的文字、口述记录，留给我们的历史叙述并不具有特别的真实性与客观性，统计数字也只是某些实体“对统计对象计数、分类、抽税、压制、改良或用别的方式塑造之的努力”<sup id="ref-zh-52"><a href="#note-52" aria-label="Footnote 52">52</a></sup>。但这并不意味着，研究历史资料会带领我们走向没有“事实”的虚无主义，我们可以层层剥开历史叙述的外壳，去思考很多问题：历史为什么会被塑造成我们如今看到的样子？文字和情绪背后是怎样的权力关系与社会关怀？记载历史究竟是为了“取回”还是“重构”、“求真”还是“控制”？……本文中，《申报》的男性叙事、苦情叙事、宏大叙事都会掩盖大量妓女群体的真实声音，雉妓的泛滥也让官方的统计数字变得单薄，但是我们通过对《申报》内容的研究，可以感知到一百年前社会资源、权力体系、话语囚笼是如何构建的，妓女群体与普通群体之间立体、弯曲、交叉的复杂关联，这最终导向了我们对人、社会、历史的关怀，在能指及其所指的意义叠加里重新自我界定，确立当代人新的自我意识与历史定位。正如贺萧在《危险的愉悦》篇首写：“我们看到的并非简单意义上的一连串‘事实’，而是它们的炮制过程；我们对‘事实’及其出笼的过程这两个方面都应关注（当然，还绝不能忘记‘我们’是谁，我们背负着什么样的历史）；……我们要做的不是寻觅‘已然在那儿’的过去，而是寻找历史记载的对象、记载历史的人和我们自己这三方面互相之间游移不定的关系。”<sup id="ref-zh-53"><a href="#note-53" aria-label="Footnote 53">53</a></sup>在能指中寻找所指，在认知中重构记忆，在关怀中反复定位，是研究《申报》妓女新闻乃至研究所有报刊新闻给予我们的启示。</p>
  <p class="bilingual-pair__en" lang="en">This is also important for modern historical research. Because of the constraints of the times, it is difficult to find textual and oral records of subordinate groups in history, leaving our historical narratives without special authenticity and objectivity, and statistics are simply the “efforts to count, classify, tax, suppress, improve or otherwise shape” of some entities.
